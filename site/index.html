    How this research proceeds
    1. Inspect two, three or more features together on the original shared scale.
    2. Identify a configuration: convergence, divergence, a band, a branch, or an additional feature varying with the collective configuration.
    3. Describe the Human and AI versions, including genre exceptions.
    4. Specify what a formula must preserve before choosing its mathematical form.
    5. Craft a pattern-specific function, potentially nonlinear and multivariate; compare its reconstruction with the graph.
    6. Evaluate the resulting augmented feature, uncertainty and recurrence on appropriate held-out data.
