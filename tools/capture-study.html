How I defined the coordinates and carried out this comparison

R04: let m be mean sentence length and σ = m × CV. The display uses x = log(1 + IQR/σ), y = log(1 + (maximum − m)/σ), with colour asinh((median − m)/σ). Mean, median and CV are query context; IQR and maximum are the joint response. Colour is fixed by that context. Zero-CV rows have undefined shape coordinates and are excluded explicitly. These transforms use the stored CSV measurements.

R06: each modifier band is represented by its radius and angle: r = hypot(ratio, dependency ratio), θ = atan2(dependency ratio, ratio). The anchors are log(1 + r/.05), θ for each band, and log mean sentence length. The response is the joint average/median/maximum depth triple. The depth plot shows median versus maximum depth, coloured by average depth. The graph-score coordinates are log median, log(average/median), and log(maximum/√(average × median)). The anchor plot holds point positions fixed and compares response colours.

Held genre and vector groups: I reused the saved diagnostic query cohort and excluded its complete 132-feature vector groups globally. For each primary genre, I also excluded the whole genre and every complete-vector group occurring in it. Training uses eligible primary genres, one representative per class–genre vector group, shared training median/IQR scaling across classes and a genre-balanced empirical response prior. Papers are descriptive queries against eligible primary training data. Genre is not a predictor.

Response models: the kernel retains the nearest 32 anchors with Gaussian weights and a bandwidth floor; the forest uses 128 trees, minimum leaf size 16 and depth limit 12. Both weight observed whole response tuples. R04 applies necessary bounded-length feasibility guards and abstains if no eligible tuple remains. R06 also compares a frozen full cubic basis with 56 columns. No authorship classifier is fitted.

Chronology: I first froze the conditional benchmark, then inspected all 18 sheets. I added graph-coordinate scoring afterwards, keeping all fitted responses fixed. This is an adaptive exploratory follow-up on the same CSV, not independent confirmation. Hosting reuses saved responses and scores; it does not refit or resample them.

Cohort accounting: R04 starts with 998 queries; one legal AI query has no compatible response tuple, leaving 997 response scores. Four further zero-CV queries are excluded from graph scoring, leaving 993. R06 has 954 queries for both score spaces. R06 excludes rows with a zero modifier-band radius.

Support limits, the product control and what remains unresolved

If δ is the query mass on branches absent from global eligible training support, every normalized law restricted to that support has TV ≥ δ and Brier ≥ δ(1 + 1/B), where B is the number of training branches. Model restrictions can raise these floors. TV − δ is bookkeeping, not a causal decomposition or an achievable optimum.

Human review depth has δ = 0.1429 and forest TV = 0.3991. AI review depth has δ = 0 and TV = 0.2713. AI review envelope has δ = 0 and TV = 0.5776. Missing support explains only part of the occupancy failure. Legal envelope TV worsens relative to the prior for both classes despite lower energy.

The R06 product control preserves each forest response marginal while removing their joint dependence. It can create new median/maximum combinations and incompatible depth ordering, so the whole-tuple support floor does not apply to it. The joint forest has lower energy in 7/10 cells, lower variogram loss in 7/10 with different exceptions, and lower TV in 4/10. Product energy is Monte Carlo estimated; its SE measures simulation precision only. Graph energy was not computed for this control.

AI abstracts provide a direct counterexample to equating score improvement with structural capture: forest log-response energy is 0.5674 versus cubic 0.5896, while graph energy is 0.9143 versus 0.8175 and TV is 0.5437 versus 0.4970. Human email and AI articles also reverse the forest/cubic graph-energy comparison.

The study establishes finite, recurring configurations and partial capture with genre exceptions. It does not establish universal separation, irreducible higher-order interactions, causation, individual authorship detection or independent-corpus confirmation.
